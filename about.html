About Us

The manufacturing & engineering arm of Axis Group in the Kingdom.

AxisSol Arabia is the KSA manufacturing and engineering subsidiary of Axis Solutions Limited, an internationally recognized engineering company with more than 27 years of experience in analytical, instrumentation, industrial automation, process optimization, environmental monitoring, and turnkey engineering solutions — with end-to-end service support from concept to commissioning and after-sales support.

Established to strengthen local manufacturing capabilities and support the Kingdom's industrial transformation, AxisSol Arabia combines global engineering expertise with local execution. Our presence enables customers throughout Saudi Arabia to benefit from faster project delivery, local manufacturing, responsive technical service support, and customized engineering solutions designed specifically for regional industries and standards.

Our capabilities span analyzer systems and shelters, own-designed sample handling systems, process automation, water and wastewater monitoring, ambient air quality monitoring, industrial buildings, electrical and control panels, IIoT-enabled solutions, and complete EPC support from design through commissioning and lifecycle services — through direct (EPC / OEM & large end users) and distribution partners (products).
